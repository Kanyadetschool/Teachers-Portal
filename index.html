Questions

Frequently asked.

I forgot my password — what do I do?▾

Ask the school admin office to reset it for you. You'll be given a new temporary password to sign in with, and can change it once you're in.

Can I use the portal on my phone?▾

Yes — it works in any mobile browser, no app to install. Everything from the register to the gradebook is built to work on a small screen.

What if I mark the wrong attendance?▾

You can correct it any time before the office locks the day's register. Tap the pupil's row again and choose the right status.

Do parents see everything I enter?▾

No — parents only see their own child's grades, attendance and notices, never the whole class or other pupils' records.

Who do I contact if something looks wrong?▾

Flag it from the portal's welfare tool, or speak to the admin office directly — either way it routes straight to them.

How your data is protected

Every change made inside the portal — a score entered, a record edited, an account updated — is written to an audit log with who made it and when. This landing page itself never writes anything to the school's database; the calendar and announcements you see here are read-only, and anything more sensitive (like the staff roster) stays locked behind sign-in.
